**Title: "AI Demystified: 10 Essential Terms Explained Simply"**

### **Final Professional Video Summary**

- **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**
  - Computers performing human-like tasks (e.g., writing emails, driving cars).

- **Language Model**
  - Predicts the next word in a sentence, generating responses incrementally (e.g., suggesting "biryani" when asked about hunger).

- **Large Language Model (LLM)**
  - Advanced AI trained on vast datasets (books, articles) capable of complex tasks like writing poetry or solving math problems (e.g., GPT, Claude, Gemini).

- **Prompt**
  - A command or question given to AI. Specific prompts yield better results; vague ones produce unclear responses (e.g., "sing *Natu Natu*" vs. "sing a song").

- **Tokens**
  - Smaller text units (words or syllables) that AI processes, affecting cost and memory limits.

- **Context Window**
  - The amount of text an AI can consider at once (like a desk’s capacity). Larger windows handle more information (e.g., GPT 5.3, Gemini, Opus 4.6/4.7).

- **AI Hallucination**
  - AI confidently generates false or misleading information—always verify its responses.

- **Fine-Tuning**
  - Customizing AI with additional data (e.g., company handbooks) to improve accuracy.

- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**
  - AI retrieves answers from external sources (like reference books) for better accuracy instead of relying solely on memorized data.

- **AI Agents**
  - AI with tool access to perform real-world tasks (e.g., ordering items, cooking) beyond answering questions (e.g., Amazon’s Rufus).

- **OpenRouter**
  - A tool simplifying AI model access via a single API.
